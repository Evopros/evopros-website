Privacy

Evopros Construction Inc. · Last updated October 16, 2026

What we collect

When you check your area or book an estimate, we receive the postal code, project type, best time, name and phone number you enter. We use them only to contact you about your project.

Website statistics

We use Google Analytics and may use the Meta Pixel to see how many people visit, where they come from, and which pages and buttons they use. These tools set cookies and receive your IP address and browser details. They don't receive the name or phone number you type into our form. You can block them with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker, and the site still works.

Who sees your details

Your request reaches our inbox through FormSubmit, the service that delivers our form. We don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email info@evopros.ca or call (343) 576-8588. Our privacy contact is Izzy at the same address.
